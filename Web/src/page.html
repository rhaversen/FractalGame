MandelbulbDeep zoom, computed in your browser

The Mandelbulb renderer of FractalGame, ported to WebGPU: a reference orbit in double-double arithmetic on the CPU, every pixel followed as a 32-bit offset from it on the GPU, with the linear series skip, rebasing and the bounding-sphere clip. Fly in until the magnification passes 1e24.
